Weather-driven wheat disease favourability

Estonian automatic weather stations (ETKI / Riigi Ilmateenistus), hourly observations 2023-10-16 → 2026-09-05. Six diseases, 30 stations, seasons 2024–2026.
Not disease forecasts. Every number is a statement about measured weather. Inoculum, cultivar resistance, previous crop, tillage and fungicide use are in no model, and the risk classes are not validated for Estonia.
